Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: support@figline.app

When reporting an issue, it helps to include your device model, OS version (iOS or Android), and what you were doing when it happened.

About Figline

Figline is a calculator you can keep notes in. Calculations stay as a tape-like history, and you can label numbers to reuse them in later calculations. Notes stay on your device. There is no account, no login, and no cloud sync. The free plan on iOS and Android shows one non-personalized banner ad from Google AdMob. Figline Pro is a subscription that removes the ad and the free limits, and keeps a daily backup on the device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?
No. Figline works without any login or account. Figline Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment.
Where is my data stored?
Notes, calculations, labels, memos, tags, and folders stay on your device and are not uploaded. Figline does send anonymous crash reports (Sentry) and anonymous usage statistics (PostHog), and the free plan sends an ad request to Google AdMob for the home-screen banner. These do not include what you write or calculate. See the Privacy Policy.
How do I turn off crash reports and usage statistics?
Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and turn off 利用状況とエラーを送信 (“Send usage and error reports”). That stops Sentry and PostHog. It does not turn off the free-plan ad. Figline Pro removes the ad.
How do I delete my data?
Delete individual notes inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove what Figline stored on your device. Crash reports and usage events are anonymous. To ask about those, use the contact on the Privacy Policy.
How do I label a number?
Write it as name = value (for example, 家賃 = 70000), or long-press a number or result to add a label. Labeled numbers can be reused by name in other calculations and recalculate automatically when the value changes.
Can I copy or share a note?
Yes. Use the copy menu in a note to copy it as plain text or Markdown, or open the system share sheet. Copy and share stay on the device. They are not a cloud link.
Is my data backed up or synced across devices?
There is no cloud sync. Figline Pro saves a daily automatic backup on the device (the latest 7 days, included in the phone’s own backup) and can export that backup as a file. Restoring from a backup file does not require Pro. Without a backup file or a device backup, uninstalling or switching phones removes the notes on that device.
What does Figline Pro include?
No ads, no limits on notes, lines, or labels, the daily on-device backup, backup export, and CSV / PDF export. It is a monthly or yearly subscription. The web app does not show ads and does not sell Pro.
