Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: support@figline.app

When reporting an issue, it helps to include your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

About Figline

Figline is a calculator you can keep notes in. Calculations stay as a tape-like history, and you can label numbers to reuse them in later calculations. Everything is stored on your device — there is no account, server, or cloud sync.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?
No. Figline works without any login or account.
Where is my data stored?
On your device only. Nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.
How do I delete my data?
Delete individual notes inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.
How do I label a number?
Write it as name = value (for example, 家賃 = 70000), or long-press a number or result to add a label. Labeled numbers can be reused by name in other calculations and recalculate automatically when the value changes.
Can I copy or share a note?
Yes. Use the copy menu in a note to copy it as plain text or Markdown, or open the system share sheet.
Is my data backed up or synced across devices?
Not in this version. Data lives on the device where you created it. Keep this in mind before uninstalling or switching devices.
