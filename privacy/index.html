Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-19
Applies to: Figline v1.0 and later

Summary

Figline keeps your notes and calculations on your device. It has no account, no login, and no cloud sync. To find crashes and to understand which features are used, Figline sends anonymous crash reports and anonymous usage statistics to two service providers. These never include what you write or calculate, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings. The free plan on iOS and Android also shows one non-personalized banner ad from Google AdMob (see Ads below); Figline Pro has no ads.

What Stays on Your Device

All notes, calculations, labels, memos, tags, and folders are stored locally on your device (iOS / Android: AsyncStorage, Web: browser localStorage). They are not uploaded to us or to any third party.

What Is Sent, and Why

  • Crash and error reports (error type, stack trace of Figline’s code, app version, OS version, device model) — to find and fix crashes. Provider: Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.).
  • Anonymous usage events (for example “a note was created”, “the copy button was used”, “a Free limit notice was shown”, with rough counts such as “2–5 notes”) — to understand which features help and where people get stuck. Provider: PostHog (PostHog, Inc.).
  • A random installation ID generated by the app — to count events from the same installation without knowing who you are. Provider: PostHog.
  • Figline Pro purchase events (iOS / Android: “Pro was bought” or “a purchase was restored”, whether it is monthly or yearly, and the country of the App Store / Google Play account, for example “jp”) — to understand how many people choose Pro, and in which store countries. Provider: PostHog.
  • Ad requests (free plan only, iOS / Android: device and app information, an ad-related device identifier that is not the IDFA, approximate location derived from the IP address, and which ads were shown or tapped) — to show one non-personalized banner ad on the note list. Provider: Google AdMob (Google LLC).

We do not send:

  • calculation expressions, amounts, or results
  • note titles, memo text, label names, tags, or folder names
  • search queries or copied / shared text
  • your name, email address, or any account information
  • prices, order numbers, transaction IDs, or purchase tokens
  • your Apple advertising identifier (IDFA): Figline never asks for tracking permission
  • contacts, photos, or precise location

We do not sell this data, and we do not combine it with data from other companies to track you. Usage statistics are sent with location lookup (GeoIP) disabled and are not linked to a person profile.

Ads

The free plan shows one banner ad at the bottom of the note list on iOS and Android. Ads never appear while you write or calculate, and never on the web. Figline asks Google only for non-personalized ads, so the ads are not chosen from a profile of you. Google still receives the ad-request information above to deliver the ad, limit how often it repeats, and prevent fraud; see Google’s privacy policy. In the EEA, the UK, and other regions where the law requires it, Figline asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before requesting ads. Figline Pro removes ads entirely.

Figline Pro Purchases

Figline Pro is a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment; Figline never sees your payment details, and has no server that stores your purchases. The app asks the store on your device whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Turning It Off

Open Settings (the gear icon on the home screen) and turn off 利用状況とエラーを送信 (“Send usage and error reports”). From then on, Figline sends neither crash reports nor usage events.

Retention

Crash reports and usage events are kept by Sentry and PostHog only for the data-retention period of our plan with each provider, and are then deleted automatically.

Deleting Your Data

  • Delete individual notes inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data Figline stored on your device.
  • Because crash reports and usage events are anonymous, we cannot look them up by person. If you want them deleted, contact us with the approximate date and we will delete what we can identify.

Children

Figline does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: privacy@figline.app

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
