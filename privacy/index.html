Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-29
Applies to: Figline v1.0

Summary

Figline v1.0 is designed to work entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or share personal data, and it has no account, server, or cloud component.

What We Collect

Figline v1.0 does not collect personal data. Specifically, it has:

  • No account or login
  • No server or external database
  • No cloud sync
  • No analytics or usage tracking sent off the device
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No third-party data sharing

Where Your Data Is Stored

All notes, calculations, labels, memos, tags, and folders are stored locally on your device (iOS: AsyncStorage, Web: browser localStorage). They are not uploaded to us or to any third party.

Deleting Your Data

  • Delete individual notes inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data Figline stored on your device.

Children

Figline does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Future Versions

A future version of Figline may add privacy-first, anonymous analytics to understand demand. If that is introduced:

  • This policy will be updated before any data is sent.
  • The App Store privacy details and Google Play Data safety form will be updated accordingly.
  • An opt-out or consent flow will be provided where required.
  • Your content — calculation expressions, amounts, note titles, memo text, label names, search queries, and copied text — will not be sent. Only event names and anonymized properties would be used.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: privacy@figline.app

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
